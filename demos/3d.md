# Demo: 3D #

IFC models open in an optional 3D view (ifc-lite),
once the two 3D files of a release are deployed to a Sharepoint folder
(setting `viewer-3d-folder`).

The screenshot shows the mock Sharepoint of the tests
(`/tests/mocks/mock-sharepoint`),
written by `just demos` after `just build`.

![IFC model in the 3D view](img/3d.png)

## Controls ##

- Mouse: drag rotates, right drag (or Shift + drag) moves, the wheel zooms, a double-click fits the model.

- Bar at the top: fit, views from above, the front, the side and at an angle, perspective or orthographic, a section plane (horizontal, front or side) with a slider,
  ↻ reloads ifc-lite (fetches the 3D files again, e.g. after new ones were deployed; also offered when the 3D view fails).

- A click selects an object:
  its IFC type, name and `#id` are shown,
  "In text" goes to its line in the text view,
  "Hide" and "Isolate" act on it, "Show all" brings everything back.

- "Properties" shows the property sets (Pset) and quantity sets (Qto) of the selected object,
  also those of its type (marked as such), values with their units;
  stays open for the next selection.
  The model is parsed for them on first use only (ifc-lite's parser).

## Settings needed ##

IFC models are previewed in 3D only with these in place:

1. The ifc-lite files of the release,
   `ifc-lite-3d.js` and `ifc-lite_bg.wasm`,
   placed together in one Sharepoint folder
   (any library or subsite, but on the same domain as the files previewed).

2. The setting `viewer-3d-folder`
   (in the settings form: "3D viewer folder")
   pointing at that folder:
   relative to the site (e.g. `Shared Documents/Viewer`),
   or from the top with a leading `/` (e.g. `/sites/Tools/Shared Documents/Viewer`).

Without them, IFC files still open as text;
the 3D view says what is missing.
The 3D view also needs WebGPU (current Chrome or Edge).
